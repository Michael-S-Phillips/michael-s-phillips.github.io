Directed graph of Mercury impact craters in which each edge records that a crater formed inside an older crater or basin. The nodes are the 31,571 craters at least 5 km across in the Herrick et al. (2018) catalog and 52 basins from Orgel et al. (2020), and the record also includes 6,299 hollows.
