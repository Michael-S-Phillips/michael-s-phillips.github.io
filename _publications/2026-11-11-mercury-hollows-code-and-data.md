Analysis code, derived data, hand measurements, and figure scripts behind the paper “Toward a Geologically Plausible Model of Hollow Formation on Mercury” (submitted to Planetary Research). The record includes the 19,110 hollow detections of Bickel et al. (2025) with recomputed positions, merged hollow fields, host-crater assignments, and the thermal and statistical tests.
