Directed graph of Martian impact craters in which each edge records that one crater formed after another, built from the Robbins and Hynek (2012) crater database using rim containment and rim overlap. The archive holds the edge list, a crater table with graph metrics, a GraphML file, and the pair-level evidence for each edge.
